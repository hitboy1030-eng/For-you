THE QUIET NIGHTS...
Where your books met my songs

Raat ki khamoshi mein tum padhai mein busy thi, aur main tumhare liye gaane gaa raha tha. Tum kitaabon mein khoyi thi, aur main tumhari duniya ke ek chhote se kone mein. Kuch khaas nahi tha us raat mein… phir bhi, woh raat mere liye khaas ban gayi. ❤️

THE LITTLE WAYS...
Food from the terrace, conversations that lasted for hours

Kabhi terrace se tumhara diya hua tiffin milta tha, kabhi COVID ke un lambe dinon mein 6–8 ghante calls par baatein hoti thi. Us waqt shayad ye sab ordinary lagta tha, par aaj wahi chhoti chhoti cheezein sabse precious memories lagti hain. ❤️
